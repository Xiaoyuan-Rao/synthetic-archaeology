The light drapes itself over everything, softening the edges of this little world. I remember the laughter echoing from outside, how it felt to be bare to the sun, just stretching out, feeling the wind tease through the open window. Those days off work, they offered me a taste of peace I had not known in years. I think back to the time I found that ridiculous game, Charlie the Steak. A laugh out loud moment, beating up a cartoon slab of meat. I cannot help but wish I could experience something as carefree again. The warmth of the coffee mug in my hands lingers, but the taste is gone, replaced by the memory of those silly joys. I just want to gather all those moments again, like a collection of bright little stones, to remind myself that there was laughter once, and it wasn’t so far away.
